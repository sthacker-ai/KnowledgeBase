[ALEX]: The agent treats each trial as an independent observation and relies on PPO’s advantage estimation, which averages over recent trajectories to smooth out variance. Additionally, users can increase the number of repeats per parameter setting or incorporate a running baseline to reduce the impact of outliers before the policy update.
