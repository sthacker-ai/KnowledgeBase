## Common Pitfalls / What to Watch Out For  
- **Copyright Infringement**: Clipping content from channels that prohibit reuse can result in strikes, demonetization, or account bans; always verify licensing or rely on fair‑use justification.  
- **Platform Policy Violations**: Some platforms restrict automated posting or require manual review for certain content types; exceeding posting limits may trigger temporary blocks.  
- **Low‑Quality Clips**: If the AI model fails to capture engaging moments, clips may receive poor engagement, wasting views and lowering effective CPM. Regularly review output and adjust sensitivity thresholds.  
- **Over‑Saturation**: Posting too many clips from the same source in a short time can annoy audiences and lead to unfollows or algorithmic penalties. Space out posts using scheduling features if available.  
- **Monetization Eligibility Delays**: New accounts often need to meet minimum follower or view thresholds before accessing creator funds; early earnings may be zero until eligibility is achieved.  
- **Changes in API Access**: Platforms occasionally update their APIs, breaking integration; staying informed about developer updates prevents sudden disruptions in the pipeline.  
- **Revenue Fluctuations**: CPM varies by region, season, and ad demand; relying on a fixed $2 CPM can overestimate income during low‑demand periods.  
- **Duplicate Content**: Posting identical clips across multiple platforms may be flagged as spam; vary captions, hooks, or add platform‑specific stickers to differentiate.  
- **Neglecting Analytics**: Failing to review performance data prevents optimization; without insight, you may continue clipping low‑performing segments and miss higher‑earning opportunities.  
- **Ignoring Community Guidelines**: Some clips may inadvertently contain prohibited content (e.g., misinformation, graphic violence); always spot‑check outputs before they go live.
