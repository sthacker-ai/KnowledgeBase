# General AI

## Overview

General Artificial Intelligence (AGI) refers to machines capable of understanding, learning, and applying knowledge across any intellectual task that a human can. Unlike narrow AI, which excels at specific, well-defined problems, AGI aims for broad, adaptable cognition that can transfer knowledge across domains. Understanding AGI is crucial for navigating the evolving landscape of AI, as it sits at the intersection of computer science, cognitive psychology, neuroscience, philosophy, and ethics. Advances toward AGI could unlock unprecedented scientific discovery, automate complex labor, and help solve global challenges, while missteps in AGI development pose existential risks.

## Key Concepts

### General AI (AGI)
General Artificial Intelligence refers to the theory and development of machines that can understand, learn, and apply knowledge across a wide range of tasks, much like a human. AGI moves beyond narrow AI (which excels at single tasks) into the realm of true cognitive simulation.

### Narrow AI
Narrow AI, also known as weak AI, is designed to perform specific tasks, such as image classification or game playing. Narrow AI systems do not possess the adaptability and flexibility of AGI.

### Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)
Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) is a hypothetical form of AI that possesses the ability to understand, learn, and apply knowledge across any intellectual task that a human being can perform.

### Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a pragmatic solution that combines retrieval systems with large language models (LLMs) to fetch pertinent documents from an external index and inject them into the generation process.

### Persistent AI Memory
Persistent AI Memory refers to the incorporation of long-term memory directly into large language models (LLMs), allowing AI systems to remember facts, preferences, and interactions across sessions.

### Large Language Models (LLMs)
Large Language Models (LLMs) are artificial neural networks that can generate human-like text based on the input they receive.
