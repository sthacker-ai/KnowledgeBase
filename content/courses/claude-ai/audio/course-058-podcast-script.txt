[SAM]: Besides routing, the course mentions effort control. How does adjusting effort change cost and quality?

[ALEX]: Effort control scales the internal thinking steps Sonnet 5 performs. Low effort uses fewer transformer layers, cutting latency and cost by about 20%; high effort adds extra reflection passes, raising cost up to 30% but improving accuracy on ambiguous prompts.

[SAM]: So we wouldn’t want to run every request at high effort just to be safe?

[ALEX]: Exactly. Running everything at high effort wastes tokens on simple queries like “What’s the capital of France?” where low effort already gives a correct answer, inflating your bill without any quality gain.
