## Background & Context
Cortisol is a hormone produced by the adrenal gland in response to stress. It plays a crucial role in the body's "fight or flight" response, helping us to respond to threats and challenges. However, chronically elevated cortisol levels can have negative effects on mental health and wellness. ## Core Concepts

### Cortisol and Its Effects
Cortisol is a hormone that helps us respond to stress. When we experience stress, our body releases cortisol, which prepares us to either fight or flee. However, chronically elevated cortisol levels can have negative effects on mental health and wellness. Research has shown that cortisol spikes can impair memory, enlarge the fear center, and make the brain feel like it's "glitching." This is because cortisol can damage the hippocampus, a region of the brain responsible for forming and storing memories.

### The Importance of Mitigating Cortisol Spikes
Mitigating cortisol spikes is crucial for maintaining good mental health and wellness. Elevated cortisol levels can lead to anxiety, depression, and other mental health issues. By managing cortisol levels, we can reduce the risk of these conditions and improve overall well-being.
