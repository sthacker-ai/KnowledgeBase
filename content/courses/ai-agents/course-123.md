## Core Concepts

### AI Agents
AI agents are software programs that can perceive their environment, make decisions, and take actions to achieve specific goals. They can be categorized as reactive, goal-based, utility-based, or learning agents, depending on their level of complexity and decision-making capabilities.

### AI Agent Skills
AI agent skills are reusable building blocks that encapsulate specific functionalities or behaviors for AI agents. These skills can be shared and combined to create more complex AI agents. Examples of AI agent skills include navigation, perception, manipulation, communication, and learning.
