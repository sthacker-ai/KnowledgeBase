AI Agents
=========

## Overview

AI Agents are software systems that perceive a goal, devise a sequence of actions, execute those actions using available tools, and iteratively refine their output until the goal is satisfied. They combine large language models (LLMs), memory systems, tool use, and goal-directed planning to automate knowledge-intensive tasks in research, business, and software development. This reference page compiles insights from eight courses on AI Agents, offering a comprehensive understanding of their key concepts, techniques, and tools.

## Key Concepts

### AI Agents

AI Agents are software systems that combine LLMs, memory, tool use, and goal-directed planning to automate complex tasks. They differ from simple rule-based chatbots in their ability to perceive goals, devise action sequences, and execute tasks autonomously.

### Large Language Models (LLMs)

LLMs are artificial neural networks trained on vast text corpora to generate human-like text. They serve as the conversational and reasoning core of AI Agents.

### Memory Systems

Memory systems in AI Agents provide persistent storage for agent state, history, and user-defined preferences. They enable agents to maintain context and consistency across interactions.

### Tool Use

AI Agents employ external tools to perform tasks in various domains, such as interacting with APIs, databases, and external services. They can also use natural language interfaces to command these tools.

### Goal-Directed Planning

Goal-directed planning is the ability of AI Agents to devise action sequences that lead to achieving specific goals. They can iteratively refine their output until the goal is satisfied.

## Techniques & Methods

### Hermes Agent Kanban

The Hermes Agent Kanban is a visual task board system for coordinating multiple AI Agents. It enables orchestration, triage, and dynamic agent assignment, allowing users to manage complex workflows with ease.

### Agent Personality and Functionality

AI Agents can be customized with personality traits to enhance user engagement and trust. By systematically embedding personality, developers can create agents that feel like "smooth operatin' mofo"s.

### AI Agent Integrations

Integrating AI Agents with external tools and services, such as databases, APIs, and software platforms, extends their capabilities and enables them to perform complex tasks autonomously.

### AI Agent Identity Files (SOUL.md)

AI Agent Identity Files (SOUL.md) externalize the agent's "self" into a separate, immutable document. This document is loaded at startup and referenced throughout the agent's lifecycle, ensuring memory, personality, and behavioral boundaries.
