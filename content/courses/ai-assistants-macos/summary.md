# AI Assistants for macOS

## Overview

AI Assistants for macOS are applications designed to enhance user experience by providing intuitive and voice-controlled interactions with macOS systems. These assistants, like TipTour, utilize advanced technologies such as Gemini Live, CUA Driver Core, and macOS Accessibility to understand the screen, listen to voice commands, and control the computer with precision. Understanding AI Assistants for macOS is crucial for maximizing productivity, streamlining workflows, and staying updated on the latest developments in the field.

## Key Concepts

### TipTour Overview

TipTour is a macOS menu bar companion that understands the screen, listens to voice commands, and controls the computer. It allows users to hold a hotkey, issue a voice command, and have TipTour point, click, type, open apps, edit selected text, or act on a freeform highlighted area.

### Voice Commands

Voice commands are verbal instructions given to AI Assistants like TipTour. Examples of voice commands include:

- "Open Apple Notes and write a short essay"
- "Change this word."
- "Move this over there."
- "Click the Blank doc"
