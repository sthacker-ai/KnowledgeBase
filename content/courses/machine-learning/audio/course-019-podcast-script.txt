[ALEX]: Speculative decoding can push a 70‑billion‑parameter LLM from roughly 15 tokens per second to over 35 tokens per second on an H100, delivering a 2.3× throughput gain, but that improvement only holds when the draft model’s acceptance rate—the fraction of its proposed tokens that the target model confirms as correct—remains above about seventy percent; if the acceptance rate drops, the target model must reject and replace more draft tokens, causing extra verification passes and memory traffic that erode the speed advantage and can even push latency back toward the baseline autoregressive case.
