## Key Concepts

### Thread Coordination

Thread coordination is the ability of AI coding agents to manage and synchronize multiple threads of execution in multi-threaded environments. This feature enables AI coding agents to work together efficiently, ensuring that tasks are completed accurately and in a timely manner.

### Synchronization

Synchronization is the process of coordinating multiple threads of execution to ensure that they are working together in a consistent and predictable manner.

### Multi-threaded Environments

Multi-threaded environments refer to computing systems where multiple threads of execution are running concurrently.
