# Understanding the Impact of Cortisol on Memory and Brain Function

## Overview
Stress can have a significant impact on our memory, executive function, judgment, and overall brain health. In this course, we will explore the role of cortisol, a hormone released in response to stress, and its effects on our brains. We will also discuss eight natural methods to help regulate cortisol levels, as suggested by a Stanford neuroscientist.

## Background & Context
Cortisol is a steroid hormone produced in the adrenal glands. It is often referred to as the "stress hormone" because it is released in response to stress, enabling the body to respond to perceived threats or challenges. However, chronic stress and consistently high cortisol levels can negatively affect various aspects of our mental and physical health.

## Core Concepts

### Cortisol and Memory
Cortisol can interfere with memory formation and recall, particularly when it comes to new information. High cortisol levels can make it difficult for the brain to consolidate new memories, leading to impaired memory function.

### Cortisol and Brain Structure
Chronically elevated cortisol levels can lead to structural changes in the brain, particularly in the hippocampus, which plays a crucial role in memory and emotion regulation. This can result in an enlarged fear center, causing increased anxiety and emotional reactivity.
