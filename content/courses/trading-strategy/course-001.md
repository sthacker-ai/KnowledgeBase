## Core Concepts

### The Problem with Breakout Trading
Breakout trading involves buying or selling assets when they break out of a specific price range or level. However, this approach can be problematic if traders and investors ignore other important factors that contribute to the breakout. The key issue is not the breakout itself, but rather the underlying conditions that led to the breakout. By ignoring these factors, traders and investors can end up buying or selling at the wrong time, resulting in significant losses.

### The Importance of Pre-Breakout Analysis
Pre-breakout analysis involves examining the underlying conditions that led to the breakout, including factors such as market sentiment, technical indicators, and fundamental analysis. By conducting thorough pre-breakout analysis, traders and investors can gain a deeper understanding of the breakout and make more informed decisions about buying or selling assets. This approach can help mitigate the risks associated with breakout failures and improve overall trading performance.

### The Role of Pivot Points in Breakout Analysis
Pivot points are a type of technical indicator that can help traders and investors identify key levels of support and resistance in the markets. By analyzing pivot points, traders and investors can gain a better understanding of the breakout and make more informed decisions about buying or selling assets. In the context of breakout trading, pivot points can help identify the key levels that contributed to the breakout and inform decisions about when to buy or sell.
