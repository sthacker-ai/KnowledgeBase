# Building a Trading System for Full-Time Workers

## Overview
This course will teach you how to build a trading system that can be effectively managed while working a full-time job. The focus will be on a system requiring minimal screen time during working hours, relying primarily on end-of-day decision-making.

## Core Concepts

### Trading System
A trading system is a set of rules and processes that define how trades are executed and managed. This can include entry, exit, position sizing, and risk management strategies.

### Minimal Screen Time
Minimal screen time refers to the limited amount of time spent actively monitoring and managing the trading system. This allows full-time workers to focus on their primary job during working hours.

### End-of-Day Decision-Making
End-of-day decision-making involves analyzing the market and making trading decisions based on the closing prices of the day. This approach simplifies the decision-making process and reduces the need for constant monitoring.

## How It Works / Step-by-Step

1. **Market Analysis**: At the end of each day, analyze the market to identify potential trading opportunities.
2. **Decision-Making**: Based on your analysis, make trading decisions for the next day. This includes setting entry, exit, and position sizing rules.
3. **Implementation**: Implement your trading decisions the following day. Ensure that your system can be executed with minimal screen time during working hours.
4. **Monitoring and Adjustment**: Regularly monitor your trading system's performance and make adjustments as needed. This can include updating your rules or strategies based on market conditions and performance.

## Real-World Examples & Use Cases
