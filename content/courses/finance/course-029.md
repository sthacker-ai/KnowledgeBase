## Core Concepts
### Swing Scanners
A swing scanner is a tool used to identify stocks that are experiencing significant price movements. It scans the market for stocks that are making new highs or lows, and alerts the investor to potential trading opportunities. Swing scanners can be used to identify both long and short trading opportunities, and can be customized to fit the investor's specific trading strategy. For example, a swing scanner can be set to alert the investor when a stock makes a new 52-week high, which can be a sign of strong momentum.

### Momentum Leaders
Momentum leaders are stocks that are experiencing strong price movements, often driven by high demand or other market factors. These stocks are likely to continue their upward trend, making them attractive to investors who want to capitalize on their momentum. Momentum leaders can be identified using various technical indicators, such as moving averages, relative strength index (RSI), and Bollinger Bands. For instance, a stock with a high RSI value may indicate that it is overbought, but if it is also making new highs, it could be a sign of strong momentum.

### 52-Week High Filter
A 52-week high filter is a simple yet powerful tool used to identify stocks that are making new highs. This filter can be applied to a universe of stocks to narrow down the list to those that are experiencing strong momentum. The 52-week high filter is a key component of the swing scanner technique, as it helps to identify stocks that are likely to continue their upward trend. For example, if a stock is making a new 52-week high, it may be a sign that the stock is experiencing strong demand, and therefore, may be a good candidate for investment.
