## Core Concepts

### TipTour Overview
TipTour is a macOS menu bar companion that understands your screen, listens to your voice, and controls your computer for you. It provides a seamless experience by allowing users to hold the hotkey, say what they want, and have TipTour point, click, type, open apps, edit selected text, or act on a freeform highlighted area.

### Voice Commands
TipTour supports a wide range of voice commands, including:
* "Open Apple Notes and write a short essay"
* "Change this word."
* "Move this over there."
* "Click the Blank document."
* "Make this line sound softer."
* "Guide me through exporting this."

These commands demonstrate the versatility of TipTour, allowing users to perform various tasks with ease.

### Modes
TipTour operates in three primary modes:
* Autopilot: TipTour performs actions for you. On by default.
* Tour Guide: TipTour teaches step by step. Off by default.
* Neko Mode: optional playful cursor mode. Off by default.

Each mode offers a unique experience, catering to different user preferences and needs.

### Controls
TipTour provides various controls to customize the user experience:
* Ctrl + Option: toggle voice mode.
* Ctrl + Shift + drag: paint a freeform focus highlight.
* Menu bar icon: open settings, permissions, and mode toggles.

These controls enable users to tailor TipTour to their specific requirements.

## How It Works / Step-by-Step
TipTour combines the following components to provide its unique experience:
1. Gemini Live for real-time voice, screen understanding, transcription, and tool calling.
2. CUA Driver Core for reliable computer control: clicks, typing, hotkeys, app launch, URLs, scrolling, and browser coordinates.
3. macOS Accessibility for native app structure and exact text/element targeting.
4. Focus Highlight for "this part" commands: hold the highlight hotkey, paint over an area, then ask TipTour to edit or act on it.

By integrating these components, TipTour achieves its goal of providing an intuitive and voice-controlled experience.
