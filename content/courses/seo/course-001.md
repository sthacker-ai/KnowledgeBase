## Core Concepts

### Business Context Loading
Before executing any SEO tasks with Grok Bot, it's essential to load your complete business context. This ensures that every prompt you run is tailored to your specific business, rather than receiving generic responses. The business context includes:
- Basic business information (name, address, phone, website)
- Service details and target markets
- Current SEO standings and goals
- Competitor analysis
- Past SEO efforts and results

This foundational step transforms Grok Bot from answering a stranger's SEO questions to providing targeted, actionable insights for your business.

### Google Business Profile (GBP) Category Audit
GBP categories are the backbone of local SEO. Your primary and secondary categories determine which searches trigger your listing in the map pack. Many businesses set their primary category once and never revisit it, missing out on valuable ranking opportunities. This course will teach you how to:
- Audit your current GBP categories
- Analyze competitor categories
- Identify high-impact category additions
- Prioritize categories based on competitor patterns

### GBP Attributes Audit
Attributes are often-overlooked tags on your GBP listing that significantly impact rankings and conversions. These include tags like "veteran-owned," "free estimates," "24/7 availability," and more. This course covers:
- Identifying missing attributes
- Categorizing attributes by importance
- Understanding the dual impact of attributes on rankings and click-through rates

### Competitor Review Teardown
Review velocity (the rate at which you receive new reviews) is a critical ranking factor. This course will show you how to:
- Analyze competitor review patterns
- Identify high-impact review keywords
- Develop a review strategy based on competitor insights
- Train customers to mention specific keywords in their reviews

### Review Response Strategy
Responding to reviews is not just about customer service—it's a ranking factor. This course teaches you how to:
- Analyze competitor review responses
- Develop a keyword-rich response strategy
- Use review responses to improve local rankings

## How It Works / Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Loading Business Context
1. **Prepare Your Business Information**: Gather all relevant business details, including basic information, services, target markets, current SEO standings, competitors, and past SEO efforts.
2. **Format the Context**: Structure your business information in a clear, concise format that Grok Bot can easily reference.
3. **Load the Context**: Paste the formatted business context into Grok Bot with the following prompt:
   ```
   "Here is everything you need to know about my business before we start any SEO work. Reference this every time I ask you to run an audit, build a strategy, or analyze competitors. Never ask me for this information again. [Insert your business context here]."
   ```
4. **Verify the Load**: Confirm that Grok Bot has successfully loaded your business context by asking it to summarize the information.

### Step 2: Executing GBP Category Audit
1. **Identify Keywords**: Choose 3 high-priority keywords relevant to your business.
2. **Run the Prompt**: Use the following prompt to audit your GBP categories:
   ```
   "Open Chrome and go to Google Maps. Search '[service] in [city]' for these 3 keywords: [keyword1], [keyword2], [keyword3]. For each search, note which of my competitors show up in the Map Pack. Then open each competitor's GBP listing and extract their primary category and all secondary categories. Put everything in a spreadsheet. One tab per keyword. Columns: business name, primary category, secondary categories, star rating, review count, ranking position. Highlight any categories my competitors have that I'm missing from my GBP. Then give me a prioritized list of categories I should add, ranked by how many top competitors have them. Start with the ones all 3 competitors share - those are non-negotiable. End with the ones only 1 competitor has - those are differentiation opportunities."
   ```
3. **Analyze the Results**: Review the spreadsheet to identify high-impact category additions.
4. **Implement Changes**: Update your GBP with the prioritized categories.

### Step 3: Conducting GBP Attributes Audit
1. **Identify Competitors**: Choose 3 top competitors to analyze.
2. **Run the Prompt**: Use the following prompt to audit GBP attributes:
   ```
   "Open Chrome and go to my Google Business Profile at [URL] and these competitors: [URL1], [URL2], [URL3]. For each listing, extract every visible attribute and tag - things like 'veteran-owned,' 'free estimates,' 'offers online appointments,' 'wheelchair accessible,' '24/7 availability,' and any others shown. Put everything in a spreadsheet. Columns: attribute name, my listing (yes/no), competitor 1 (yes/no), competitor 2 (yes/no), competitor 3 (yes/no). Highlight every attribute my competitors have that I'm missing. Then create three separate lists: Attributes ALL top competitors have - these are table stakes, I need them immediately. Attributes 2 out of 3 competitors have - these are strong recommendations. Attributes only 1 competitor has - these are differentiation opportunities. For each missing attribute, tell me the likely ranking impact (high/medium/low) and whether it also affects click-through rate."
   ```
3. **Analyze the Results**: Review the spreadsheet to identify missing attributes and their potential impact.
4. **Implement Changes**: Update your GBP with the high-impact attributes.

### Step 4: Performing Competitor Review Teardown
1. **Identify Competitors**: Choose 3 top competitors to analyze.
2. **Run the Prompt**: Use the following prompt to teardown competitor reviews:
   ```
   "Open Chrome and go to these competitor GBP listings: [URL1], [URL2], [URL3]. For each competitor, read their last 50 reviews. Extract: Total review count. Average star rating. How many reviews in the last 30 days. How many reviews in the last 60 days. How many reviews in the last 90 days. The most mentioned services in reviews (list top 5). The most mentioned neighborhoods or cities (list top 5). The most mentioned staff names if any. Any recurring complaints or negative themes. The top 5 keywords and phrases I should train my customers to mention in reviews based on what's working for competitors."
   ```
3. **Analyze the Results**: Review the extracted data to identify patterns and high-impact keywords.
4. **Develop a Strategy**: Create a review strategy based on the insights, including training customers to mention specific keywords.

### Step 5: Developing Review Response Strategy
1. **Identify Competitors**: Choose 3 top competitors to analyze.
2. **Run the Prompt**: Use the following prompt to analyze competitor review responses:
   ```
   "Open Chrome and go to my GBP listing at [URL] and these competitors: [URL1], [URL2], [URL3]. For each listing, extract the last 50 review responses. Put everything in a spreadsheet. Columns: business name, review text, response text, response length, keywords in response, sentiment of response. Highlight any patterns in competitor responses - common keywords, phrases, sentiment. Create a list of the top 10 keywords and phrases competitors use in responses that I'm not using. For each missing keyword/phrase, tell me the likely ranking impact (high/medium/low) and whether it also affects click-through rate."
   ```
3. **Analyze the Results**: Review the spreadsheet to identify high-impact keywords and phrases.
4. **Develop a Strategy**: Create a review response strategy that incorporates the high-impact keywords and phrases.
