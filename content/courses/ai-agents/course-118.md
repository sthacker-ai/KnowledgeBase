## Core Concepts

### AI Agents
AI agents are software entities that perceive their environment and take actions to achieve specific goals. They can be programmed to perform various tasks, from simple, repetitive actions to complex decision-making processes.

### Cron Jobs
Cron jobs are a type of time-based job scheduler in Unix-like operating systems. They allow users to schedule tasks (commands or scripts) to run automatically at specific times or intervals.

### Job Description
A job description is a document that outlines the roles, responsibilities, and expectations for a specific position in a company. In the context of this course, each AI agent has its unique job description, which defines its tasks and objectives.
