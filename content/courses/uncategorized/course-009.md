Core Concepts
-------------

### Mission Control

Mission control is a dashboard that provides an overview of your entire system, including content, agents, ideas, performance, and more. By integrating Hermes into your mission control, you can see what is happening, what is waiting for you, what is being blocked, and what needs approval. This way, Hermes becomes a part of your operating layer, rather than just another chat app.

### Web Hook Triggers

Web hook triggers allow you to automate tasks by setting up triggers that initiate specific workflows. For instance, when you move a video idea to your "to film" list in Notion, Hermes can automatically create a filming brief for you. This way, you can ensure that your agents follow you around where you already live and work, without any manual intervention.

### Hermes Kanban Board

A Hermes Kanban board is a visual representation of your workflows, which can be integrated directly into your mission control dashboard. By using a Kanban board, you can see the tasks that Hermes is working on, instead of digging through chat history. This helps Hermes become a part of your operating layer, rather than just a chat app.

### Cron Jobs

Cron jobs are time-based tasks that Hermes can perform automatically. By setting up cron jobs, you can have Hermes wake up at specific times and perform tasks, such as sending you an AI story worth reacting to, scanning websites for fresh posts, checking for outlier videos, auditing content boards, and summarizing content ideas.

### Sub-Agents

Sub-agents are specialized agents that can be used to perform specific tasks. For instance, you can have Hermes run three sub-agents for video research: one for vidIQ and keyword signals, one for YouTube outliers and transcripts, and one for your channel performance and Nova memory. By using sub-agents, you can have multiple threads of work, which can be separated and managed more effectively.

### Slash Goals

Slash goals are objectives that you can set for Hermes, rather than just asking for a single response. By using slash goals, you can provide Hermes with an outcome, sources, constraints, and deliverables, which will help it work towards a specific goal. For instance, you can ask Hermes to decide the strongest Hermes agent video that you should film this week, using vidIQ YouTube outliers, competitor transcripts, your posted videos, and Nova memory, while avoiding repeated angles and generic AI guru framing.
