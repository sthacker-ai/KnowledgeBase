## Core Concepts

### Vibe Coding

Vibe coding is the feeling of writing code, which can be incredibly satisfying and rewarding. It involves the process of creating software solutions using programming languages and development tools. Vibe coding is a crucial concept in software development, as it highlights the importance of the developer's experience and satisfaction in the coding process.

### Agentic Engineering

Agentic engineering is the process of building AI agents to automate tasks, such as coding. It involves designing, developing, and deploying AI agents that can perceive their environment, make decisions, and take actions to achieve specific goals. Agentic engineering is a powerful concept that can revolutionize various industries, from software development to manufacturing.
