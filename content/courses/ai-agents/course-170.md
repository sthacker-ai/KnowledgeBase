### **The Maya Civilization and Timekeeping**
The Maya civilization is renowned for its sophisticated understanding of time and astronomy. Unlike the Gregorian calendar, which introduced leap years to account for the Earth's orbit around the Sun, the Maya developed a day-count system that could track time over millennia with remarkable accuracy. Their calendar, based on vigesimal (base-20) place values, included units like the *k’in* (day), *winal* (20 days), *tun* (360 days), and *k’atun* (7,200 days).
