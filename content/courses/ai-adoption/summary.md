# AI Adoption

## Overview
AI adoption is the process of integrating artificial intelligence technologies and systems into an organization's operations, culture, and decision-making processes. It is a critical topic for modern businesses, as AI can offer significant benefits, such as improved efficiency, better decision-making, and enhanced customer experiences. However, AI adoption also presents challenges, including ethical considerations, security risks, and the need for new skills and organizational structures. This reference page summarizes the key concepts, techniques, and insights from the course "AI Adoption Framework: A Structured Path to Responsible and Secure AI Implementation."

## Key Concepts

- **AI Adoption Framework:** A five-stage model developed by Microsoft that provides a structured path to responsible and secure AI implementation. The framework includes the following stages: Strategy, Plan, Readiness, Governance, and Operations.
- **Strategy:** The first stage of the AI Adoption Framework, where organizations define their AI vision, objectives, and success metrics.
- **Plan:** The second stage, where organizations create an actionable plan that aligns with their AI strategy, including defining use cases, identifying data sources, and selecting AI technologies.
- **Readiness:** The third stage, where organizations assess their technical and cultural readiness for AI, including data quality, infrastructure, and skills.
- **Governance:** The fourth stage, where organizations establish policies, procedures, and accountability structures for AI implementation and management.
- **Operations:** The final stage, where organizations operationalize AI, including deployment, monitoring, and continuous improvement.

## Techniques & Methods

- **Data Quality Assessment:** A technique for evaluating the quality and suitability of data for AI applications.
- **AI Ethics Framework:** A framework for ensuring that AI applications align with ethical principles and guidelines.
- **AI Governance Framework:** A framework for establishing policies, procedures, and accountability structures for AI implementation and management.
- **AI Skills Development:** A method for developing the skills and knowledge needed for AI implementation and management, including training and education programs.

## Insights & Lessons Learned
