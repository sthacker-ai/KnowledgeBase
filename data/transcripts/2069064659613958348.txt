Have you ever pasted a Loom to an agent and realized, ah, agents can't do anything with Loom links? Well, there's a solution now. I built an open-source agent-native clone of Loom called Clips. You record and share them exactly like you do a Loom, but from the link, any agent can directly see the transcript, see what was on the video at any timestamp, and pull images at any point based on what you're saying. In short, it can see and hear what's on the video. You can also import a Loom just from a URL, or upload a video file or audio file. So now when people send you stuff, you can just pass it directly to your agent and not have to worry about it. It's totally free and open source. You can start using it in the browser right now or download the desktop app. And if you try it, I'd love to hear your feedback.
