## Core Concepts

### AI Agents
AI agents are software programs that can perform tasks autonomously, making decisions based on data and algorithms. They can be classified into two main categories: narrow or weak AI, which is designed to perform a specific task, and general or strong AI, which is capable of performing any intellectual task that a human can.

### Local AI Models
Local AI models refer to AI models that are run on a local device, such as a computer or a mobile phone, rather than on a remote server. This approach provides faster processing times and better data privacy, making it an attractive option for many applications.

### Robotics Engineers
Robotics engineers are professionals who specialize in designing, building, and integrating AI into physical devices. They play a crucial role in developing AI agents that can interact with the physical world, such as robots, drones, and self-driving cars.

### Marketers Who Know How to Build Distribution
Marketers who understand how to build distribution channels are essential in getting AI agents to the right audience. They can help create awareness, drive adoption, and ensure that AI agents are used effectively to solve real-world problems.

## How It Works / Step-by-Step
To set up and manage AI agents, follow these steps:

1. Define the problem or task that you want the AI agent to perform.
2. Choose the type of AI agent that is best suited for the task, such as a narrow or weak AI.
3. Design and build the AI model, including the algorithms and data structures.
4. Integrate the AI model with the local device or hardware.
5. Test and refine the AI agent to ensure it performs as expected.
