# How to Monetize Boring AI Tools with Google AdSense

## Overview
In this course, you will learn how to set up a Google AdSense account and integrate it with your website to monetize your AI tools. The tutorial focuses on creating a mortgage calculator app, but the principles can be applied to any niche.

## Background & Context
The idea of building multiple small AI tools and monetizing them with ads is a proven strategy for generating passive income. By creating a large number of tools, each targeting a specific keyword, you can attract a steady stream of visitors and display relevant ads to them. Google AdSense is a popular ad network that allows you to earn money by displaying ads on your website.

## Core Concepts

### Google AdSense
Google AdSense is a free program that allows you to earn money by displaying Google ads on your website. When a visitor clicks on an ad, you earn a small commission. AdSense is a popular choice for website owners because it is easy to use and offers a wide range of ad formats.

### AdSense Account Setup
To start using AdSense, you need to create an account and provide some basic information about your website. This includes your website's URL, payment country, and account type. Once you've entered this information, you can start setting up your payments and connecting your website to AdSense.

### Connecting Your Website to AdSense
To connect your website to AdSense, you need to verify your site ownership. This involves adding a small piece of code to your website and clicking a verification button in AdSense. Once your site is verified, you can start displaying ads on it.

### Ad Management
After your site is connected to AdSense, you can manage your ads through the AdSense dashboard. This includes choosing the ad formats and sizes that you want to display, setting up custom channels to track the performance of your ads, and linking your AdSense account to other Google services.

## How It Works / Step-by-Step

1. **Create a Google AdSense account.** To create an account, go to the AdSense website and click on the "Sign up now" button. Follow the prompts to enter your website information, payment details, and other required information.
2. **Set up your payments.** Once your account is created, you can set up your payments by clicking on the "Payments" tab in the AdSense dashboard. Here, you can enter your payment address, choose your payment method (e.g., direct deposit, check, or wire transfer), and set up any tax forms that may be required.
3. **Connect your website to AdSense.** To connect your website to AdSense, you need to verify your site ownership. To do this, click on the "Sites" tab in the AdSense dashboard and then click on the "Add site" button. Follow the prompts to add your website's URL and copy the verification code. Next, go to your website's HTML code and paste the verification code between the `<head>` and `</head>` tags. Finally, go back to the AdSense dashboard and click the "Verify" button.
4. **Choose your ad formats and sizes.** Once your website is connected to AdSense, you can choose the ad formats and sizes that you want to display. To do this, click on the "Ads" tab in the AdSense dashboard and then click on the "Content" tab. From here, you can choose from a variety of ad formats, including text ads, display ads, link units, and more. You can also choose the size of your ads and customize their appearance to match your website's design.
5. **Set up custom channels.** Custom channels allow you to track the performance of your ads by grouping them into categories. For example, you might create a custom channel for your homepage ads, another for your blog post ads, and so on. To set up custom channels, click on the "Ads" tab in the AdSense dashboard and then click on the "Custom channels" tab. From here, you can create a new channel and add the ad units that you want to track.
6. **Link your AdSense account to other Google services.** If you use other Google services, such as Google Analytics or Google Search Console, you can link them to your AdSense account to get a more complete picture of your website's performance. To link your AdSense account to other Google services, click on the "Integrations" tab in the AdSense dashboard and follow the prompts.
